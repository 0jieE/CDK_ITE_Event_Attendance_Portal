Install {{ ui.brand_title }}

Installing puts the portal on your home screen and opens it full-screen like an app.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Tap the Share button (bottom of the screen).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen .
  3. Tap Add.
Android (Chrome / Edge)
  1. Open the browser menu ⋮ (top right).
  2. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Confirm with Install.
Computer (Chrome / Edge)
  1. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the menu and choose Install ITE Attendance.
  2. Click Install.
